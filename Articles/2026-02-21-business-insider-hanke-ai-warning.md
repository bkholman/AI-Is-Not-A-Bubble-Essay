## Summary

Business Insider reports economist Steve Hanke’s warning that enthusiasm for AI may exceed its capabilities and economic prospects. Hanke endorses Yann LeCun’s criticism that fluent language generation can obscure limited understanding of the physical world, while acknowledging that chatbots have practical uses. He links the investment outlook to whether AI companies can deliver their ambitious revenue forecasts. The article places his skepticism alongside Michael Burry’s concerns about chip obsolescence and Jeremy Grantham’s comparison with earlier technology bubbles. It also presents the opposing view that productivity gains and profits will justify investment, citing continued fundraising and infrastructure spending. The warning combines doubts about LLMs as a route to human-level intelligence with financial risk; neither claim establishes that all AI applications will fail.
