## Summary

ABC News reports that Australian data-center operators raised at least A$35 billion in 2026 to date, citing Reserve Bank of Australia staff analysis obtained by the broadcaster. Much of the funding was debt, raising questions about exposure if AI investment disappoints. The report also describes how construction, skilled-labor demand, and electricity infrastructure can add inflationary pressure before AI delivers wider productivity gains. However, the evidence is mixed: long leases and physical collateral help explain debt financing, and the central bank found little evidence that data-center borrowing had worsened financing conditions for other Australian businesses. The article documents rapid expansion and potential vulnerabilities without concluding that AI is a fad or that an economic crisis is inevitable.
