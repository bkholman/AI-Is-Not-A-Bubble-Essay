## Summary

CNBC reports that investor Michael Burry now expects a possible AI market reversal sooner than he previously anticipated and has adjusted bearish positions accordingly. His concern centers on whether revenue can sustain the sector’s enormous capital spending and interconnected commitments. Citing research from Ares Management, he suggests a disappointing revenue season could cause corporate boards to redirect investment, undermining assumptions supporting the boom. He also points to expanding Chinese memory-chip capacity as a potential challenge to semiconductor pricing. The article presents an investor’s forecast, not evidence that a collapse has begun: Burry has remained bearish while technology stocks reached new highs, and the timing of any reversal remains uncertain.
