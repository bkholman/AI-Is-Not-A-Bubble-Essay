## Summary

Forbes contributor Ingmar Rentzhog argues that an AI investment reversal could become a financial crisis if it coincides with energy and climate shocks that damage the same lenders and borrowers. He highlights debt, private credit, data-center commitments, and fossil-fuel infrastructure as channels through which disappointing AI demand could transmit losses. Higher fuel, food, and insurance costs could simultaneously weaken households and businesses, turning investment losses into a broader credit squeeze. This is a conditional stress scenario rather than a prediction of inevitable collapse. Rentzhog acknowledges that cheaper gas, adequate AI revenues, continued lending, and central-bank liquidity could interrupt the cascade; he does not identify a single bank carrying all the correlated exposures he describes.
