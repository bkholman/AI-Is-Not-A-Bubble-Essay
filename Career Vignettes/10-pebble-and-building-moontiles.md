# Pebble and building Moontiles

I received a Kickstarter Edition Pebble smartwatch in May 2013. When I could not find a watchface I liked, I wrote my own: Moontiles.

I wanted it to show the moon phase. The Pebble API did not have the floating-point math support I needed for the calculations, so I generated five years of data and stored it in the app. I worked around a limitation to build the experience I wanted.

The feature that mattered most to me was the connection to my iPhone’s notifications. A glance at my wrist helped me decide whether something needed attention without pulling out my phone. The screen was modest, but the battery lasted for days.

I was using and building for this technology before it had become commonplace. That is part of what being an early adopter has meant in my life.
