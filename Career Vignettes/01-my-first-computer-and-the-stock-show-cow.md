# My first computer and the stock-show cow

In the summer of 1982, when I was eleven, I used the $500 I had earned selling a cow at the stock show to buy my first computer, a TRS-80 Color Computer. My mom thought it would be a waste of money.

I started programming, and I could see where this was going. I remember thinking: this is the future. I did not want to be a farmer like my dad. I wanted to do this.

That purchase was an early example of a pattern that has followed me through my life: seeing a possibility, spending my own money to explore it, and finding that other people could not yet see what I saw.
