# The inflection point I see

I recognize myself in the early adopter described in Geoffrey Moore’s Crossing the Chasm. I have seen laptops, smartphones, and smartwatches move from unfamiliar devices into everyday life. The [homepage essay](https://old2.brianholman.com/) I wrote years ago explored that same pattern: the gap between an early market and mainstream adoption.

Everything we have done with the web, and everything else we have built along the way, gives me a frame of reference for this moment. Even with that experience, what we are about to do with AI feels like an inflection point on the scale of moving from the Stone Age to the Industrial Revolution.

The level of intelligence and partnership is amazing. This is next level. I am sure other AI systems will continue to come along as well.

If you are looking at this and seeing only something overblown, I think you are missing the opportunity. Get involved. This is going to transform the world.

AI is not a bubble.
