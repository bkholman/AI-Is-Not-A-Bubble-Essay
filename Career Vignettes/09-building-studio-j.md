# Building Studio J

At [Close To My Heart](../Professional%20Background/06-ctmh.md), I built the Studio J digital scrapbooking platform from scratch. We spent millions of dollars on research and development.

We brought in a private R&D shop to supplement our internal team as we worked to get the platform out. Even with that help, we were probably about a year behind schedule. We had a grand vision, but turning it into a working product was extraordinarily difficult.

When I look at what I can accomplish with AI in a few weeks, I am comparing it with that experience: the money, the teams, and the tremendous effort it took to bring Studio J to life.
