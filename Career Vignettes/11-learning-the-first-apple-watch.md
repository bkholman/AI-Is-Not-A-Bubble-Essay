# Learning the first Apple Watch

When Apple entered the smartwatch market, I became an early adopter again. The build quality and screen impressed me, but I found the watchfaces limiting and had to learn when to use the screen, the crown, or the side button.

At a Home Depot self-checkout, I waved my wrist a couple of times without success. Eventually I read the instructions and learned that I had to press the button twice to bring up Apple Pay.

That little episode captures another part of early adoption: the rough edges are real. I can be excited about a technology and still have to learn how to use it. In my [2015 homepage essay](https://old2.brianholman.com/), I was hopeful about Apple’s ability to bring wearables to a broader audience, while leaving the outcome open.
