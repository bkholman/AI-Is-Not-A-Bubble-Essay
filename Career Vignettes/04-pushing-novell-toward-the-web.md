# Pushing Novell toward the web

I was recruited to [Novell](../Professional%20Background/02-novell.md) when it was a networking giant—a billion-dollar company whose technology was used by organizations of every size.

As a web architect, I spent four years pushing to bring the company into the web age. We built Shop Novell, developed e-commerce, worked to make NetWare more web-aware, and created an intranet that was recognized as one of the best at the time.

I wanted the web embedded in what the company did. From my perspective, Novell was too attached to its past and did not have the passion for innovation that I hoped to find.

I was eventually recruited to [WebMiles](../Professional%20Background/03-webmiles.md) and moved into the startup world. That experience at Novell stayed with me: seeing a direction clearly does not mean an established organization is ready to move with you.
