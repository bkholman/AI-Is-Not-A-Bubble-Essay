# From Palm Pilot to a smartphone

I was an early adopter of the Palm Pilot. When Palm brought that kind of software into a phone with the Treo, I was on the bandwagon early again.

For me, that was another inflection point. Something I already found useful was becoming part of a device I could carry and use as a phone. I could see the direction things were moving, and I wanted to be part of it.
