# BYU executive MBA: the first iPhone in class

When I went back to BYU for my executive MBA, I had already been an executive for seven years. I remember coming to class after buying the first iPhone.

I do not remember exactly what I paid. In my memory, it was something like $800, and people thought the price was outrageous. My MBA classmates had their BlackBerrys. Their reaction was: Brian, you are crazy. Why did you spend so much money on this iPhone?

My answer was: guys, trust me. The iPhone is the future.

That is the kind of moment I mean when I talk about being an early adopter. I could see what the device might become before the people around me felt a reason to switch.
