# Apple Vision Pro at launch

I bought Apple Vision Pro at launch. It is still a work in progress, and it is still too heavy. But it is amazing.

I believe it represents the future—or at least one aspect of the future. I can see that potential while recognizing what still needs to improve. That has been a recurring part of my experience as an early adopter.
