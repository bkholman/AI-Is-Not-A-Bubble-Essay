# PointeCast and the cost of building

At [PointeCast](../Professional%20Background/05-pointecast.md), we raised millions of dollars in venture capital and developed e-learning platforms to deliver online training through the web.

Building those systems was painful work. I know what it takes to turn a product idea into something that works, because I have done it. That experience is the comparison I bring to what I am doing with AI today.
