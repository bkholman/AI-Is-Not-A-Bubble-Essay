# Conducting an orchestra of exceptional engineers

I feel like the conductor of an orchestra, with the best hundred-person orchestra in the world available to me. I can sit down with my AI agents, work with ChatGPT-6 Astra, and ask how to move forward.

I have worked with engineers who were twenty to fifty times more productive than other engineers. You paid a premium for that talent. Some were also divas, and their behavior could make the rest of the team unhappy or less productive.

Now it feels as though I have an entire team of those exceptionally productive engineers, and I get to orchestrate the work myself. That is the level of partnership I am trying to describe. It is extraordinary.
