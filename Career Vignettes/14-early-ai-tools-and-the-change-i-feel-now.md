# Early AI tools and the change I feel now

I had been using AI for a while before this latest change. I used early tools in JetBrains for coding and related work. They helped, but they could also be maddening.

Sometimes it felt like beating my head against a wall. I would have to tell the tool something twenty times before it finally got it right.

My experience with ChatGPT-6 Astra is different. The level of intelligence feels game-changing. Having lived with those earlier frustrations, I can feel how much the partnership has changed.

I have founded companies, raised venture funding, and worked deeply in technology. I am bringing all of that experience to this comparison. This is a new level of capability.
