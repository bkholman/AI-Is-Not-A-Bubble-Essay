# A year of work in less than a month

Working on my own with ChatGPT-6, I have been able to accomplish in less than a month what I believe would previously have taken me a year with a team of about fifty people.

I am talking about something that feels like a hundredfold increase in productivity. I do not have to wait for a developer to become available or work through the same pushback before I can move forward.

I know the work, and I know what it would have required in the past. The difference I am experiencing is game-changing.
