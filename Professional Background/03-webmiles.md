# 03 WebMiles, Inc.

**Dates:** May 2000 – April 2002

**Role(s) as listed:**

## Company context

Creator of a universal airline miles loyalty rewards program that let members earn miles through partners including Kroger, Avis Car Rental, Wyndham Hotels, the WebMiles Credit Card, and online retailers. Received $40 million in venture capital funding.

## Experience summary

Developed a scalable loyalty rewards management platform and online application handling millions of consumer transactions from hundreds of partners. Features included customer and partner self-service, online affiliate integration, data analytics, and email marketing.
