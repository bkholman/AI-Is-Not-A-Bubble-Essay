# 02 Novell, Inc.

**Dates:** October 1996 – May 2000

**Role(s) as listed:**

- Web Systems Architect, Global Technical Architecture Group, Information Technology
- Webmaster, Electronic Marketing

**Location(s):** Provo, Utah; San Jose, California

## Company context

At the time, Novell was one of the largest and oldest independent software companies, with annual revenue of approximately $1 billion. Its products included the NetWare network operating system and Novell Directory Services identity management system.

## Experience summary

Migrated Novell’s corporate web infrastructure from a monolithic system to a redundant n-tier architecture supporting millions of hits per day. Architected the deployment of Novell’s direct-sales online store and deployed mission-critical web systems on pre-release Novell software. Partnered with software engineering teams to enable Novell’s operating system, directory, and application server to support enterprise web applications. Transformed Novell’s intranet from static pages into a business system supporting the daily work of its global workforce. The intranet received the 1999 CIO WebBusiness 50/50 Award, recognizing it as one of the top 50 corporate intranets.
