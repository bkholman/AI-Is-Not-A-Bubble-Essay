# 05 PointeCast Corporation

**Dates:** November 2003 – August 2007

**Role(s) as listed:**

- President and Chief Executive Officer, Member of the Board of Directors

**Location(s):** Lindon, Utah

## Company context

A leading global provider of rapid online training software and services

## Experience summary

Founded the company in November 2003 as a spinout from [Geolux](04-geolux-communications.md). Secured multimillion-dollar venture capital funding. Combined internal R&D with technology acquisitions to develop products. Architected PointeCast’s learning management system to manage, deliver, and track SCORM-compliant online multimedia training. Acquired a key technology component from Cisco Systems for our PowerPoint-based e-learning authoring tool. The company received a U.S. patent on adaptive e-learning. Formally launched the product in 2005. Served over 1,000 customers worldwide, including ConEdison, Dupont, General Electric, Halliburton, NuSkin, Pitney Bowes, and the U.S. Military. Achieved average year-over-year revenue growth of 293% during the final three years as an independent company. Generated over 40% of revenue from recurring subscriptions. Used a low-touch online sales and marketing model and developed channel partnerships. The company was acquired in June 2007 by Helius, a Hughes company, in a private transaction valuing it at over seven times revenue.
