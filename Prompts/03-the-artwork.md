# Making my essay more visual

I want to make the essay more readable and enjoyable by giving readers something to see alongside the stories I tell. My career has unfolded through tangible technologies and products, and I want the artwork to make those experiences concrete. Use the current essay and career vignettes to decide where each visual belongs.

The Dezota website repository may be consulted for source assets. I want local copies of the images in the `Assets/` folder so the essay does not depend on remotely hosted images to render. Reuse my existing smaller AI Is Not a Bubble cover image beneath the byline. Add a representative TRS-80 Color Computer photograph after the story about buying my first computer with the money from my stock-show cow. Add a Tandy Model 102 photograph after my BYU history-class story. 

Add a representative NCSA Mosaic screenshot after my account of discovering the graphical web. It should help readers understand why that moment was so striking. 

I want Studio J illustrated with my finished Moab family scrapbook spread from my Dezota biography. The website’s local asset can be copied into this repository. Place it after the Studio J paragraph in the essay and also in the Studio J career vignette. 

Link my reference to *Instant JavaScript* directly to [my book page](https://www.dezota.com/about/leadership/brian-holman/instant-javascript/). Link the essay’s description of Studio J as patented, and the Studio J vignette’s related-patents reference, to [my patents page](https://www.dezota.com/about/leadership/brian-holman/patents/). Include [my Dezota biography](https://www.dezota.com/about/leadership/brian-holman/) among the essay’s closing links.

For the conductor metaphor, I want an original illustration that conveys what it feels like to bring my experience and judgment to an extraordinary orchestra of capability. 

Keep the captions short and connected to my stories. They can carry my voice: a cow becoming a computer and a different future; yellow legal pads beside my Tandy and a hundred words a minute; the graphical web making possibilities visible; a grand vision that remained difficult to execute; and experience I can now put to work.
